FluentAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-27. Replace support contact before production launch.

Who we are

FluentAI (“we”) provides a language-learning mobile app with AI tutoring. Contact: privacy@fluentai.app (configure SUPPORT_EMAIL / DNS to match your domain).

Data we collect

  • Account email and password hash
  • Learning settings (languages, level, daily goal, LLM consent)
  • Conversations, messages, vocabulary you save
  • Content reports you submit
  • On-device: profile display name, avatar image URI, practice streaks

How we use data

To authenticate you, personalize tutoring, enforce AI quotas, and improve safety. After you consent in-app, chat content is sent to our language-model provider to generate replies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Processors may include our database host, email (SMTP) provider, and OpenAI (or configured model provider) for tutoring and moderation.

Age

FluentAI is for users 13+ (or the minimum digital consent age where you live). We require age confirmation at signup.

Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete account) or follow account deletion instructions. Server-side account data is removed; model providers may retain data per their policies.

Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt). Sessions use short-lived access tokens and hashed refresh tokens. Transport should use HTTPS in production.
