Delete your FluentAI account

You can delete your account and associated data from the FluentAI mobile app: Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete account. You will be asked to re-enter your password.

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your email and password credentials
  • Language settings and onboarding preferences
  • Conversations, messages, and saved vocabulary
  • Refresh sessions and password-reset tokens
  • AI usage counters and content reports you submitted

Local device data (Keychain tokens, on-device profile/avatar/streaks) is cleared when you delete or log out from the app.

If you cannot use the app, email privacy@fluentai.app from the address on your account. We verify ownership and complete deletion within 30 days.

Chat content may have been processed by our language-model provider to generate tutor replies. Provider retention follows their policies after deletion from FluentAI systems.
